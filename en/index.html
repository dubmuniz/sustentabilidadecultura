    The Institute

    Instituto Sustentabilidade e Cultura (Institute for Sustainability and Culture) is a Rio de Janeiro-based organisation that connects research, culture and sustainability to strengthen social and environmental impact initiatives.

    We work at the intersection of academia, public policy and civil society. Our experience comes from international academic research on urban governance, environmental justice, race and culture, and from hands-on fundraising for science, innovation and cultural projects.

    We help companies, collectives and institutions turn what they already do into competitive proposals: we map opportunities, shape narratives and support applications from start to finish.

    What we do

    ◎

    Opportunity radar

    Ongoing mapping of national and international calls, awards, grants and accelerators, prioritised by fit.

    ✎

    Applications and narrative

    Proposal writing, theory of change, budgets and reusable materials that tell a consistent story.

    ❖

    Research and evaluation

    Assessments, qualitative research, impact evaluation and advisory services for cultural, social and environmental projects.

    02

    Bruno Barboza Muniz

    Sociologist · Project coordinator · Fundraising

    Sociologist and project director working on urban governance, environmental justice and public policy in Latin America. He holds a PhD in Sociology from the London School of Economics and Political Science (LSE), supervised by Juan Pablo Pardo-Guerra and co-supervised by Mike Savage and Paul Gilroy.

    He was a postdoctoral researcher at the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ) (2016–2017) and at the Centre for Social Studies (CES), University of Coimbra (2017–2022), funded by the European Research Council (ERC) through the POLITICS project on the politics of anti-racism in Europe and Latin America. He has taught as a guest lecturer in the Graduate Programme in Social Anthropology (PPGAS) and at the School of Fine Arts (EBA) at UFRJ.

    At Humana Povo para Povo Brasil, where he has worked since 2016 and serves as Statutory Director of Projects, he has raised nearly R$ 4.5 million and coordinated strategic projects in education, solidarity economy, sustainability and social innovation, with partners such as UNDP, MRV Engenharia and the Bahia state government. His expertise includes budget management, impact indicators and building partnerships with public bodies and private companies.

    Since 2026 he has coordinated international fundraising at Fiotec, the foundation that supports Fiocruz (Brazil’s Oswaldo Cruz Foundation), and he directs the Institute for Sustainability and Culture.
